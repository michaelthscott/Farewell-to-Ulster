<h1 class="post-title">Voodoo</h1>
</header>

mix sperm and tobacco\
store it under the bed

film container slipped \
inside the hessian\
bumped by Mum's vacuum cleaner

the interrogation begins\
drugs it must be drugs\
but no I insisted no\
but no more could be said

how explain \
a game called\
pseudo scientific \
immagination
