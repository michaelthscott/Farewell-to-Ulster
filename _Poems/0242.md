<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Kerrygold</h1>
</header>

take the butter\
from the fridge\
put the last bit on the dish

fold the buttery wrapper\
Kerrygold

put it in the cold cupboard\
in another dish

baking cakes\
angelica\
licking the bowl
