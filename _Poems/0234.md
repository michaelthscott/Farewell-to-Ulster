<h1 class="post-title">Cave Hill</h1>
</header>

up at the top of the zoo\
is a small desolate boating lake

up there you can see the cave \
at the foot of Cave Hill

all those animals \
dumb or miserable

I'm rarely ever up here\
top of the Antrim Road

Napoleon's Nose smooth and sad\
accidental coincidence of bumps

place in shadow\
North Belfast
