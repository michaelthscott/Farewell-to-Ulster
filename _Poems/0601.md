<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Scrap bucket</h1>
</header>

he who leaves the house at night\
to empty the scraps

first hedge\
second hedge\
paving stones

windmill over my head\
hoping nothing drops

upended\
top of the compost heap\
whack

rush back
