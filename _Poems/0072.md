<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Runaway</h1>
</header>

PGO\
got to go\
got to get out of here

no one home\
on the run\
dropout scruffy gear

Stranraer ferry\
windy gloomy\
queasy sudden fear

forgot passport\
bound to get caught\
back across the Irish Sea
