<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Empty house</h1>
</header>

the house is empty\
because he's dead

Mummy and me\
sorting shirts

old dead jackets\
on the bed

i'll have this\
i'll have that

sea island cotton\
from another age

destined one\
to wear it out
