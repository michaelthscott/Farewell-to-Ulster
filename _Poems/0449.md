<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Matisse</h1>
</header>

blue and white rug\
back of the hall door\
cutout Matisse La Mer

first when they got married\
they lived with his parents\
while they found the field\
and built the house\
her father designed

let's call it Longshot

Grandpa Rip had a sort of breakdown\
and started clawing at the walls\
when the rendering didn't set

fine white sandy\
house icing\
flecked with broken\
sea shells
