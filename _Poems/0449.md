blue and white rug\
on the hall door\
Matisse cut out La Mer

Teddy and Noel's early marriage\
feels like i almost was there

first when they got married\
they lived with his parents\
while they found the field\
and built the house\
that her father designed

let's call it Longshot

Grandpa Rip had a sort of breakdown\
and started clawing at the walls\
when the rendering didn't set

fine white sandy\
house icing\
flecked with broken\
sea shells
