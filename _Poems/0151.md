<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Prison</h1>
</header>

have to go to school\
have to go to school

middle aged\
adults owners\
make all the rules

I hate to go to school\
hate to wear a uniform\
hate to study do exams

wish I could be me\
could be free
