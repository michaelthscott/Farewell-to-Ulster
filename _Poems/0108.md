<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">The Giant’s Ring</h1>
</header>

famous\
signposted\
empty

dolmen\
caveman's kennel\
cemented\
littered

the ring mound\
round\
uneventful

couple of visits\
enough

more potent\
lures the towpath\
the Lagan

empty windows of linen history
