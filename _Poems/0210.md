<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Fish</h1>
</header>

rain spreading its rings\
across a flat black lake

fish will be rising now\
steady pull in the line

stealthful finger tip\
and wrist looping

figures of eight\
cradled in palm

then whish\
whip back\
the rod

wrist again
