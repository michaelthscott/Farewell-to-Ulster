<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Fish</h1>
</header>

rain spreading rings\
across the flat black lake

fish will be rising now\
pull in the line

stealthful finger tip\
and wrist looping \
figures of eight\
cradled in my palm

and then whish\
whip back\
the rod

wrist again
