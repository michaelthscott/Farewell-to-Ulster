<h1 class="post-title">Corduroy</h1>
</header>

I see a coat\
and love that coat\
I clearly want to be in that coat\
to be the one who owns that coat

perfect thick tan corduroy

knotted silk scarf\
hand carved walking stick\
before the window an artist stood

I know how that coat would become me
