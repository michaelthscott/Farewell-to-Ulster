<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Vincent</h1>
</header>

storyteller's delight\
captures reader's delight

it's an interesting failure\
he tried

clumsy shaggy dog\
younger brother's loyalty\
I met him on a Dutch train

Le Grand Meaulnes\
shot himself in the stomach\
ate paint
