<h1 class="post-title">Another brother</h1>
</header>

Hilda and Rip\
just arrived home\
Noel ringing from the hospital

Dunhill smoke in the corridor

Harold and Sarah\
fuss over Christopher\
Teddy's going to lose another tooth
