<h1 class="post-title">Goodbye Mr Hall</h1>
</header>

Mr Hall \
knows it all\
knows how to \
make us love him

so much so\
a tiny scold\
floods heart \
with shame

giant face\
over tiny desk

leaving soon\
tall windows room

going to teach in Malaysia
