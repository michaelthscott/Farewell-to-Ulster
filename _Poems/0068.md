<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Three large paintings</h1>
</header>

once in the middle of the night\
blue white glacier on the stairs\
fell with smash wake up clatter

behind above mum's desk\
the warm Lake Como view

chalky green and sharp\
trees shaped like leaves\
pre-war Lagan reflection
