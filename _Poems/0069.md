<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Frogspawn</h1>
</header>

out Inchmarlo back gate\
straight down Mr Peskett's lane

nature walk \
hunting for tadpoles

squat down \
big Martian brain

frogspawn\
huddle of best friends

poke the thing \
with a stick

scoop up \
jam jars of goo
