title: Frogspawn
---
out Inchmarlo back gate\
straight down Mr Peskett's lane

nature walk \
hunting for tadpoles

squat down \
big Martian brain

frogspawn\
huddle of best friends

poke the thing \
with a stick

scoop up \
jam jars of goo
