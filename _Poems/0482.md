Daddy's a factory manager\
we must be middle class

we all go to a grammar school\
yes we're middle class

Mum and Dad from the Malone Road\
said no to golf and tennis\
still middle class

plenty of art books in our house\
culturally middle class

don't have a lot of money\
but still middle class

beautiful garden in the summer\
house built especially for us

it's a funny word that cordial\
Mum makes our orange juice

welcome to the middle class
