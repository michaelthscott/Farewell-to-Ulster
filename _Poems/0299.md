title: George Woodcock
---
anarchism makes an exciting sound\
anarchism creates another world\
idealist antagonist exciting sound

anarchism dismissed from the beginning\
anarchism forgets people\
nihilists are young self destructive\
Proudon Bakunin Kropotkin\
no one has names like that\
Belfast has no dreams
