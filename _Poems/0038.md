title: Shame
---
saw you in the sunday papers\
beautiful stainless steel watchstrap

Mum’s gift \
Grandpa Rip's Rolex\
slim sleek and slender fit

perfect in the papers\
but too easily bent\
sadly stupidly broken\
yes I broke the clasp

fearful of rebuke \
ashamed of clumsiness\
stonewall Drumbeg bridge \
looking at the surface of the Lagan

say goodbye \
let you go\
later lie \
say you broke and fell

sleep well in the mud\
Grandpa Rip
