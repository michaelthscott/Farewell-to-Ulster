<h1 class="post-title">Queen's Film Theatre</h1>
</header>

QFT\
i cycled miles to get here

QFT\
find the mews\
find the door

QFT\
wait in the long white corridor

QFT\
ponder programmes\
Leaud's hair

QFT\
saw Weekend\
drums

QFT\
riding back
