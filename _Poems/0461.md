<h1 class="post-title">Failure</h1>
</header>

Barron & Co's\
going to make\
simple shopping bags

factory manager\
asks his son to draw\
simple design of a flower

rigid outline sign\
no chance to be popular\
Noel instead made a rose

nothing came of either
