<h1 class="post-title">Newcastle upon Tyne</h1>
</header>

take easy option\
Christopher there

go with Simms\
to the interview

some third years \
live next to Chris

front door friendly\
quick to adopt us

caring Alison Winkle\
escaping her name

hero Dave Critchley\
pop fringe over jeans

later back home\
letter accepted

Simms gone
