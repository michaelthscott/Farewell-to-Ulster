<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Mummy's hair</h1>
</header>

Mummy's hair was thick and brown\
wavy from a tortoise shell clip

Daddy liked it like that

Mummy's hair is shorter now\
no falling curls at the back

Mummy's gawping at the TV

Mummy Daddy three boisterous boys
