<h1 class="post-title">Lough Erne</h1>
</header>

Augher Clocher Filemiletown\
Mum and Dad arranged\
to put our caravan \
in a field

grey farmhouse\
drive slowly through their yard

farm buildings seem so dead\
no life about them\
even when people come out

little beauty here\
except what I find \
with hunting eyes\
but don't

fairy tree in the next field\
view of gap in the hedge

green yellow white and blue\
masterpiece still wet \
in the car boot

Noel saw\
through gap \
distant blue tree
