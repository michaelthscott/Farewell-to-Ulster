<h1 class="post-title">Lough Erne</h1>
</header>

Augher Clocher Filemiletown\
Mum and Dad arranged to put our caravan in a field\
grey farmhouse\
drive slowly through their yard

farm buildings seem so dead\
no life about them\
even when the people come outside

no beauty here\
except what i might find with my hunting eyes\
but don't

drab ghost

the fairy tree is in the next field

with the view of the gap in the hedge

green yellow white blue\
masterpiece still wet in the car boot

through the gap in the hedge to the distant blue tree
