<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Mr Musenger</h1>
</header>

sinking feeling \
climbing the stairs

impervious intractable\
the ancient old dentist

Wellington Place \
behind window net\
oblivious busy below

that devilish drill arm\
grinding wheels \
and dark cord

Heath Robinson\
torture scene
