<h1 class="post-title">Giggle</h1>
</header>

a horse\
in a field\
we're at Tollymore\
seen from the side\
grazing

half embarrassed \
lean on the fence\
no actual discussion

in my mind awareness grows\
of that thing that is an instrument

almost touching the grass
