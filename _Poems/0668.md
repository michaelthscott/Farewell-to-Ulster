<h1 class="post-title">Independent</h1>
</header>

I'm runnning up\
running up\
through the castle\
through the tower\
to the top\
nobody with me

I can see the Rhine\
and the car park\
and the fields\
and the trees\
the whole wide adventuring world

where have you been
