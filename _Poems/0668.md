<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Independent</h1>
</header>

I'm running up up\
through the castle\
through the tower

to the top \
the very top\
nobody but me

down there the Rhine\
the car park fields and trees\
a whole wide adventuring world

where have you been
