<h1 class="post-title">Instruction</h1>
</header>

high up\
in Mr Auld’s RE\
Religious Education \
room

where I planned\
to burn the school down\
lit candle in petrol saucer

hung high up there\
Gauguin’s Mysterious Water

lean jungle figure \
the source
