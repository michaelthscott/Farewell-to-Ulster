<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Ingenuity</h1>
</header>

on the landing by the stairs\
hear the car if it arrives

big glass Venetian vase \
between two pillows

Vaseline in the hole \
and poke it

pillows away wash up vase back \
on the dressing table where \
it came from
