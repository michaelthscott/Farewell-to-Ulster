<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Paintings</h1>
</header>

Daddy puts his pictures on the wall\
and others that he has bought

Basil Blackshaw\
Noreen Rice\
Colin Middleton\
Gros

some I know when they were painted\
others date from long ago\
Tosa Lagan Como

Grandpa Rip's old master

some of mine in the playroom\
and Tim's nail thing
