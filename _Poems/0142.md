Mum near the kitchen\
Dad at the head\
against the wall

looking down the table\
and the room\
boys next to Mum\
I bags the end\
apex\
side for one\
on my left\
Granny and Grandpa Scott

later\
on my left\
Grandpa Scott

that's Daddy's Dad
