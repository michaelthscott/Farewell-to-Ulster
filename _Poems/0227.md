<h1 class="post-title">Long grass</h1>
</header>

furry head long hand caress\
some grass has a knuckle

slips out of stem when you pull it\
fresh nutty moment gets chewed

hay has a dry scratchy rustle\
prickly rolling down the hill

flattened long grass nest\
our hideout lookout fort

the whole field\
damp at the roots
