<h1 class="post-title">Television</h1>
</header>

first television lives\
bottom of the trolley\
plug and aerial\
curled at the back

wheeled into the \
centre of the room\
when we want to \
see something

sometimes just children's\
programmes like Andy Pandy\
and the Flowerpot Men

other times we all sit down\
to watch drama or the News

finally got\
new colour one\
bigger with legs of its own

squats in everyday corner\
we watch it all the time
