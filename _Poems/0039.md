<h1 class="post-title">Dropout</h1>
</header>

I'm a notebook\
not a textbook\
and I won't cram it in

I'm a learner\
not an earner\
if I swot it's a sin

I'm a big lig\
not a dead twig\
nor the head of a pin

I'm a dropout\
not a cop out\
let the battle begin
