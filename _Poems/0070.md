<h1 class="post-title">Inst front gate</h1>
</header>

out the front gate of Inst\
turn left down the street\
past Smithfield or\
through Smithfield\
car park gravel bus station\
hard men coming down\
from Shankill and Falls

I never watch\
rugged hair\
top buttoned\
tight denim jackets\
hands in pockets\
hunched

swagger sullen walk of the prols\
yoiks from The Time Machine\
where is Divis Flasts\
22 or 24 home
