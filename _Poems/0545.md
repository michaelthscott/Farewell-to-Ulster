<h1 class="post-title">Abrupt</h1>
</header>

strange how the road turns\
right before the big lychgate

hurtling down the long straight hill\
the driver heaves the bus around

Drumbeg's not really a place\
no shop just a church \
and a bus stop

on that same right angle bend\
Bristow's drive up to big house \
old money

this is where the Malone Road ends\
after Lady Dixon's and the golf club \
across the Lagan

there's something ancient here\
gone missing
