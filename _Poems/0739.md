Dad keeps important things\
stored in the deed box

about shoebox sixe\
with a lay-flat handle \
chipped gloss black\
and gold paint lines\
small key for the lock

he also has a carrying case\
with drop-in hanging folders\
here things are filed away \
newspaper clippings
