<h1 class="post-title">Lisburn Road Library</h1>
</header>

clear huge room full\
shelves and shelves

books \
tall books\
big books

picture books\
subjects to choose

shiny red yellow blue\
slightly tattered spines

pink yellow blue \
small envelopes

open two sides \
keeps the ticket

librarian lady loves her\
neat rows of borrowing
