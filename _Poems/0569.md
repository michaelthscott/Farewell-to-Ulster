dropping out\
going to Leeds

beside the Ripper's hotel\
Romanowich with his Lee\
me and Speedy room each

give Speedy's brother my bike

Rimbaud Sims turns up \
tale of escape Istanbul

stays with some girl he knows

reunited trio\
didn't last long\
soon fell apart

talk is not glue
