<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Leeds</h1>
</header>

drop out\
go to Leeds

beside the Ripper hotel\
Romanowich with his Lee\
Speedy and me room each

Speedy's brother gets my bike

Rimbaud Sims appears  \
Istanbul tale of escape

stays with some girl he knows

the reunited trio\
didn't last long\
soon fell apart

talk is not glue
