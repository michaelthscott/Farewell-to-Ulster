farewell to Ulster\
so matter of fact

when speaking your mind\
there's no need for tact

a spade is a spade\
and a black crow is white

sure other's opinions\
are never quite right

farewell to Ulster\
there's no more to say

i seen what you're up to\
i'm going away

your mountains are molehills\
your accent is flat

i'm off to London\
to see where that’s at
