title: Bedroom
---
feeling bedspread weave\
open palm and fingertips

hefty brown portfolio\
slid in under the bed

identical bedspreads both beds\
Welsh blankets from Granny Scott

alone in my room\
read those biographies

woodchip papered ceiling
