the car comes slowly up the path\
come for lunch you father's here

charcoal grey suit\
middle of the day routine\
hair slightly greasy at the back

there and back\
the factory there and back

Billy Cascadden's loyalty

carport wood stacked\
home and role\
whole life

middle of the middle life\
the food routine

nested in the food routine\
the yes the no the private life
