<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Marching orders</h1>
</header>

finish up Sunday lunch\
time to get some air

Grandpa Scott single\
only on his own now

tumble out\
open car boot\
stomp into our wellies

winter route march\
trudge run along\
muddy walk  \
afternoon

drive home\
past sunset
