<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Marching orders</h1>
</header>

finishing Sunday lunch\
it's time to get some air

Grandpa Scott \
on his own now

tumble out\
open the car boot\
stomp into our wellies

route march winter\
run trudge along\
muddy walk  \
afternoon

drive home\
past sunset
