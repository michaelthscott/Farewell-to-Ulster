clinging to the edge\
legs against the wall\
out of my depth

who's he\
won't move\
pushing past me

under\
spluttering\
legs kick nothing\
arms flapping\
swallowing

chlorine in my nose

grab the side again\
frightened\
clinging

back towards the shallow end
