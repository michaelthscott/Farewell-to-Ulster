<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Young couple</h1>
</header>

the things Mum and Dad did\
before I was born\
the Matisse Cut Outs rugs\
the big diagonal squares\
on the doors in the playroom\
the furniture from Heals\
their honeymoon

the young couple\
the grandchild\
happy bucktoothed grandmother\
Daddy pleased his mum

I came\
number two\
the Rippingham one
