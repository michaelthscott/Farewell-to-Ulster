<h1 class="post-title">Speedy</h1>
</header>

interminably telling a joke\
long after all guessed \
the punchline

held us transfixed\
carrying a rary bird to\
the bottom of the garden

in a wheelbarrow\
and reflected\
casually

it’s a long way to tip a rary
