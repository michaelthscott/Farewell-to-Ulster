dovetail\
mortice and tenon

pencil metal ruler\
score the line

not quite a perfect fit\
bang it with the mallet

dark brown woodworking room\
me Erwin Shaw and Trevor Munn\
swapping lines from last night's\
I'm Sorry I'll Read That Again
