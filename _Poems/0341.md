out on the football pitch\
behind the portacabins

Simms' Hitler Course sign\
easels stools piled up

Speedy emptying\
screenprint cleaner

photographing\
smashing chaos\
unbridled bad boy chaos

climbing into the empty concrete shell

the new building\
sitting on the floor\
romanticising ourselves
