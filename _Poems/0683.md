<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Outside</h1>
</header>

places I find\
when alone\
in nature

where wind sun\
birds insects show\
there's a wide world\
where question of food\
would be problematic

whole Ulster\
social fabric fungus\
house family home thing\
patchwork fields and roads\
bogus not in the world\
just a way to ignore it\
lets us view nature \
not belong in it
