title: Chess
---
Grandpa Rip knows\
how to set out his chessboard

smelly old pipe\
fruitcake for tea\
it's yours if you ever defeat me

Signal Corps\
First World War\
Martello towers\
stuck for hours\
next move sent by semaphore
