<h1 class="post-title">The car</h1>
</header>

two in the front\
everyone else in the back\
everybody facing the same way

our metal box\
got a motor and four wheels\
we're going for a drive on Sunday

I like to watch\
the side of the road\
hedgerow\
hedgerow\
hedgerow

I spy with my little eye\
something beginning with\
animal vegetable or mineral
