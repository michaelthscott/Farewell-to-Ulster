<h1 class="post-title">The Factory</h1>
</header>

Mr Noel

your father went into the factory at 15

bales of jute

family business is tragic

Donegal Road to the car port\
how many thoughts\
end to end\
reach from the Donegal Road\
to the car port

a car port's not a garage\
it doesn't have a door

Uncle Gerry's not my uncle\
he's really Great Uncle Gerry\
but no one says Great Uncle

the things that come from\
the factory back to Longshot

tea chest\
cardboard spindle\
tractor wheel spool\
hessian sheets for the garden

where Daddy goes in the morning\
why he comes back at night
