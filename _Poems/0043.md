<h1 class="post-title">Responsibility</h1>
</header>

in a clearing on a log\
Mum explains to me how she thinks

your father and I will have to get a divorce

I can't do anything right\
he criticises everything I do

is she going to cry

I'm a bit young for this\
not much I can do\
but listen

I'm the one she told
