title: Peach Melba
---
peach melba \
smooth glossy tinned peaches

Mum’s homemade \
confectioner's custard \
vanilla ice cream

fresh from the garden \
raspberry sauce full of pips

all \
sliding down \
past the insides \
of both my cheeks
