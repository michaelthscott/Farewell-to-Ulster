<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Girlfriend</h1>
</header>

Mum's selling the house\
so everyone's come\
to say goodbye to the old days

Ethel's sat by the fireplace\
in charge of herself

tell me Michael\
have you got a girlfriend yet
