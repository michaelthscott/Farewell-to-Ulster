<h1 class="post-title">War games</h1>
</header>

building a battlefield\
war gaming\
mound\
war gamer's battle\
war gamer's magazine

my farming game\
field rolled out\
tractor planting little wooden cubes\
it doesn't work

the feeling of the war game world\
war gamers in a world apart
