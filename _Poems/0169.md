<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">In the fields</h1>
</header>

hedge ends\
fields merge

boggy bit\
rushes clump

hoof holes\
cow clap

crusty cake\
orange flies

passing talking looking down\
pushing our way through the boggy bit
