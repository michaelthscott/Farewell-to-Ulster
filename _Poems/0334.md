<h1 class="post-title">Hamster</h1>
</header>

on the table in the playroom\
its warm sawdusty smell\
the cage the wheel

Timothy's hamster\
crawls up shoulder\
its bony little paws

huddled\
messy\
dumb

one morning huddle\
blind purple babies\
next morning \
they're gone

why do hamsters \
eat their babies

weak little squeak of the wheel
