title: Hamster
---
on the table in the playroom\
with warm sawdusty smell\
the cage the wheel

Timothy's hamster\
bony little paws \
crawl up shoulder

huddled\
messy\
dumb

one morning \
blind purple babies\
another morning \
they're gone

why does the hamster eat its babies\
weak little squeak of the wheel
