<h1 class="post-title">Wandering vast land</h1>
</header>

in\
little\
bike-riding \
patch of green\
I read of long roads

time to get a haircut\
slap the dashboard

Dean plays the piano\
maybe same as Speedy\
simply mythological\
was Kerouac
