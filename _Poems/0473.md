<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Coracle</h1>
</header>

hunter trapper\
red indian canoe\
pitch poured over linen

made up my curragh model\
upturned cutting wave prow\
rowlocks peg long slim oars

a coracle would just go\
round and round and \
I'd get nowhere
