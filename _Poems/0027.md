<h1 class="post-title">Delicious</h1>
</header>

cake mix\
finger licks

white glass\
Kenwood bowl

sugary buttery joy

in the top shelf jar\
lurks rare angelica\
stringy crystal green

fingertip roll\
cake icing dot\
hard damp silver ball
