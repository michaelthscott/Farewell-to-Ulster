<h1 class="post-title">Friendship Book</h1>
</header>

purple Christian concoction\
packed bland with snippets

religion's definitely pernicious \
using bland thoughts as a lure

to which I have natural \
adamantine resistance

but a thought for each day\
is an intriguing idea for a book
