<h1 class="post-title">Digging</h1>
</header>

do it with a spade\
cut the sod off\
that's right\
with your foot\
dig it in and under

there's a slow tearing sound\
as exposed grass roots\
lose their grip on the ground

you can sometimes find\
a shard of pottery
