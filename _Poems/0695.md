<h1 class="post-title">God</h1>
</header>

God is an old man with a white beard no\
made the world in seven days no

Mary is a virgin no\
Jesus son of God no\
Jesus resurrected no

Christmas religious no\
Christian country no

Catholic not Protestant yes\
Protestant not Catholic yes
