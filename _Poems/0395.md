<h1 class="post-title">Wooden window frames rot</h1>
</header>

Daddy got absorbent strips\
come in a roll and laid\
along the frame

bone dry all day\
but damp at night\
weaping condensation

sometimes don't \
soak all the water

puddles lurk behind \
the playroom curtain\
while we watch tv
