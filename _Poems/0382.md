<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Feet</h1>
</header>

shoe lace broke\
frayed ends

tie it\
skip a few holes

shoe lace broke

black school shoes\
scuffed

occasionally polished\
dented toe

inside my shoe\
sweaty fluff
