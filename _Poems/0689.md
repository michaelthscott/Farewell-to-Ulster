<h1 class="post-title">Skipper</h1>
</header>

Mummy's friend Jean Rabjohn\
they were sea rangers together\
looks like a sergeant major dog

makes pottery\
like Mum would like to do

it's my present for passing the 11+\
a trip to London

Carnaby Street velour \
and kipper ties
