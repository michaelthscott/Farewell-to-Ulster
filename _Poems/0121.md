<h1 class="post-title">Peat streams</h1>
</header>

peat streams clean gravel\
rocky pools cut the bog

fly tie wanderer\
rod in hand\
lands a little fish

dumb gasp\
gawps at me

wet hand quivers\
unhooking sadness
