<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">After lunch</h1>
</header>

looking out the car window\
front seat next to my father\
drive into Belfast after lunch

bungalows behind beech hedges\
on this down ridge Belfast begins\
passed the end of Dunmurray Lane
