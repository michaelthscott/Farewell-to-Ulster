<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Toboggan</h1>
</header>

found myself\
unintentionally\
by myself

empty house\
snow\
window\
bedroom window\
looking out\
garden field hedge trees\
hill\
toboggan hill

the breath\
all of us behind\
the Dorritiy's\
Bonar Barry\
Tim and Chris\
everyone tumbling\
sliding\
ice steel\
steel ice\
but i wouldn't do it\
afraid uncomfortable\
proud

i'll learn on my own\
trek across the field\
to the hill\
learning on my own

alone on the hill\
not afraid on my own\
down\
did it
