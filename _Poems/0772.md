title: Homemade cream
---
solid kitchen top\
not ordinary wood

dark grained teak\
thicker than my thumb

corner by the back door\
bolt the cream maker\
under the overlap

chrome cow tail\
rocking churn\
pinhole and spout

force through\
hot milk and melted butter

President\
unsalted

from Sawyers\
beside the Bank Buildings
