<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Kink</h1>
</header>

walk up between the protacabins\
sun throws my shadow ahead

hair almost down \
to my shoulders

damn kink\
right side\
over my ear

old brown pullover\
that shrank\
a little

slightly flared\
bottle green\
needlecord

scuffed laceups\
that come up\
past the ankle

that kink\
I just can't\
get rid of it

still stoned from last night
