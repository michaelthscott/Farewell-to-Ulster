<h1 class="post-title">Alan Ladd</h1>
</header>

cow pats mud tufts of grass\
I hate the cold out of doors\
rather be by the radiator\
slumped watching TV

Yank black and white \
gangster movies in\
the afternoon

how I hate standing\
around in the cold

rugby is hell
