<h1 class="post-title">Bohemia</h1>
</header>

in Granny Scott's\
big maroon armchair\
I'm watching BBC

Modigliani velvet suit\
leaps on a gravestone\
spouts an insatiable Art

living room bookcase\
Phaidon high up books\
investigate all afternoon

Central Library\
Dewey 920 bio\
no need school

thumb in palette\
mix the colours\
turpentine smell

what to paint\
my imagination\
house and trees
