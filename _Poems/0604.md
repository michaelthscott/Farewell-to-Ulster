<h1 class="post-title">Family car</h1>
</header>

Daddy got a new car\
a white Vauxhall

Daddy chose a Saab\
Saab's are Swedish\
safe well designed

sports car racing car\
toy car vintage car\
Mum's little Mini

we never had a station waggon\
I've never seen a Lamborgini

the Deux Chevaux\
jumped sideways\
over the hedge

rust
