<h1 class="post-title">Shaman</h1>
</header>

black plastic crow caller\
let’s out a skraaaaaughk\
pointless bird talker toy\
farmers call them \
to kill them

got somewhere in Switzerland \
my neat little alpine horn whistle\
letting out this long feeble weeep\
dangling split ring from lanyard
