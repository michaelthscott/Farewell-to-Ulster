speedreader\
ordered through the post\
brown plastic\
shutter window flashing\
lines of text

skim the line\
read faster

never worked

words are mulled over\
communed with\
enjoyed

GBS prefaces at lunchtime\
top floor\
English block\
by the window\
at the radiator\
looking down on the quad
