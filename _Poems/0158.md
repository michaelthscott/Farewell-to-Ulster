here's your pocket money\
don't spend it all at once

sixpence in the slot

penny\
3d

twelve shillings in the pound\
a guinea's already obsolete

doubloons\
treasure chest

two and six

have you ever seen a 50 pound note\
there's supposed to be a 100 too

bus fare

fondling my replica Confederate currency\
smells of mothballs
