title: Why did you cut your hair
---
<nav class="breadcrumb">

<a href="/Farewell-to-Ulster/ErasRefactor/0009.html">Rupert Stanley</a>

huge John Lennon poster\
hair cut surprisingly short\
frizzy thinning scissor halo

cut mine too\
now that I've left school\
no more rules to protest about

just teenage long hair \
conventions to be broken
