<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Nausea</h1>
</header>

sunday papers make me sick\
print smell mixed with car seat

picked up after church\
gloomy shop in Hillhall

they get me glasses\
but never wear them

nausea glamour\
colour supplement
