title: Malone Road Golf Club
---
slow curve of the drive\
edge of a dull pond

silently dutiful grandfather \
takes his grandson fishing

crunch of posh gravel \
old war memorial plaque\
glimpse into the Club House

lonely glum old Grandpa Scott\
lifts rods and bags from the boot

push out a miniature white boat\
drift in circles around the pond

drift cast drift\
catch nothing

Granny Scott loved to play golf\
wore spiky shoes with a flap
