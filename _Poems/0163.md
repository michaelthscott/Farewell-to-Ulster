<h1 class="post-title">Lollipop man</h1>
</header>

shiny white overcoat\
plastic bag on his hat

happy lollipop man\
dripping rain drops

stop children crossing\
sign laid on the hedge\
makes a miniature handbag\
from a Senior Service pack

thank you in the middle of the road
