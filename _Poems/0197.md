square red book\
six years in white\
dematerialization of the art object

ideas events recorded\
detailed documentation\
photographs\
pale black and white

1973\
Lucy Lippard\
the context the manner

leaving behind\
it's a sculpture

line of sticks and\
torn grass

walking back later\
with Heather\
to find it
