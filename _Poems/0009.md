<h1 class="post-title">Picnic blankets by the shore</h1>
</header>

borrowed dog\
took off like a rocket

and me\
feet flapping over\
sandy turf burrowed isle\
shouting rabbits rabbits\
like Barry says to Tina\
to drive her wild

rabbits rabbits

and the borrowed\
mad straining panting\
sniffing terrier shoots \
straight down a burrow \
me clinging to the dog lead\
dragging it out by the dog lead

and then \
and then he \
dived down the hole

me reporting\
to the grown ups
