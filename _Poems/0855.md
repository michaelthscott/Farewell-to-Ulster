I imagine huge pike lurk \
at the bottom of the lake

eyes up \
wary \
of the bright spinning lure

I'll catch one but \
I never caught one

transparent mirror carp \
bottom feeding catfish\
wild salmon never a chance

overstocked rainbow trout\
the occasional perch tiddler\
uneventfull unsatisfying success

gradually the rod bags\
loiter in the cupoard

I go fish in the library
