<h1 class="post-title">Strange menace</h1>
</header>

I’m a dalek

we go \
ex\
ter\
min\
ate

blindest toilet plunger eye\
wheelchair under a bumpy bucket\
kitchen utensil ray gun

give us a break\
watched it anyway
