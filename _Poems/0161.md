<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Socks</h1>
</header>

socks up\
boy scout\
long woollen grey

white elastic garter\
two cross cut green ribbons

socks up\
long grey\
black and yellow\
Inchmarlo stripes

pulling his socks up

over knee\
turn down\
over garter

long trousers

long grey\
no more\
socks up
