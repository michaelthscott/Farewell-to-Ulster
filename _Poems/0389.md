<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Giant hogweed</h1>
</header>

look \
it’s a giant hogweed\
pale branching monster\
rising above riverside leaves

it escaped you know\
it’s poisonous \
from Kew

heads turn\
following the monster\
riding above the stonewall curve
