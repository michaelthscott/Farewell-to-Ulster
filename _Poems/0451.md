<h1 class="post-title">Drawing</h1>
</header>

I can draw\
with my pencil\
shade with 4B

open the little short blade\
of the my penknife and \
sharpen the end\
to a point

HB 2B 4B

new sheet\
my drawing pad\
empty white what
