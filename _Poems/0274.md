Noel's wearing his grey suit\
shirt and tie

walking towards the car\
getting out of the car closing the car door\
getting something out of the boot

Noel wears his grey suit\
to the factory\
to the office\
Monday to Friday

Noel wears his charcoal grey suit\
to the office\
to the factory

driving down the Malone Road\
driving down the Lisburn Road

on the Hillhall Road\
on the Ballylesson Road\
picking up the car keys\
folded cotton hanky in his pocket

Hush Puppies\
Clarks Shoes

in the winter\
he wears a dark brown sheepskin coat
