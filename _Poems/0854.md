<h1 class="post-title">Suspicious</h1>
</header>

somehow ended up late \
leaning on the bar\
with a tutor

Alistair the argumentative one\
trying to provoke me \
puts his finger\
in my ear

startled me\
knowing what it means\
he thought I thought he knew
