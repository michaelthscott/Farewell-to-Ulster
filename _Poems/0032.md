<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Sore feet</h1>
</header>

Grandpa Scott has got sore feet\
it's terrible Teddy they're tingling

the house is so empty since Sara died\
pulls at his big brown shoe

put them against the radiator Harold\
crikey Grandpa Scott's crying
