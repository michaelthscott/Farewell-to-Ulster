<h1 class="post-title">Germ of elsewhere forming</h1>
</header>

Sunday afternoon\
let's go for a drive

window handle\
the winderuper\
it's a little loose\
fingers wobble it

nose and cheek\
against the glass\
not really interested\
in farms and hedges
