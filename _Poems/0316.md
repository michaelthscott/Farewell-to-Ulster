<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Wild rovers</h1>
</header>

local boys\
past the house\
to get to the Glen

in through the bushes\
up the river\
it's private land\
but ok for us to play here\
local boys

old gingerbread house\
stream through the lawn

no one around\
maybe a dog

a nod\
a smile\
local boys
