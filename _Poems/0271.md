<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">The dinner hall</h1>
</header>

acrid smell\
school dinner cooking\
no peace round the table

baked bean shepherd's pie\
custard and apple crumble\
stodgy horrors

loner\
tuck shop\
tooth decay
