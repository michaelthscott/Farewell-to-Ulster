title: Fancy dress
---
little smudgy greasepaint sticks\
smear a bit like lipstick

my birthday party fancy dress\
Mum dressing me as a gypsy

the dressing up box

big old cardboard box\
crammed with costume bits and pieces\
long ago Mum and Dad \
dressed as cowboys

never occurs to me \
until opening the front door\
she dressed me as a girl
