title: Tactile
---
Granny's Scott sofa\
feels hard but plush

tightly curled \
deep warm wool\
like claret astrakhan

I sit here well behaved\
and feel it with my hand\
as the television shows\
Modigliani in grey velvet\
shouting in a graveyard
