<h1 class="post-title">Murderer</h1>
</header>

down there on the lawn\
on the curved edge\
of the front drive\
my arm tight\
full nelson\
tighter

really got him struggling\
puffy gasping black out

got carried away \
almost killed him

shocked\
separating us\
I wasn't punished
