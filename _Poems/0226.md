<h1 class="post-title">Heading west</h1>
</header>

cross the Border\
wicked witches appear\
twisty bends for half a mile

verge crumbles faces peer\
number plate says\
from up North

Granny Scott says Connemara\
sounds like the start of a song

Grandpa Scott says Glendalough\
sounds like he's changing a tyre

ice box lumped between \
restless back seat monkeys

Mum reaches back\
peeled apple slap \
separate the foes

Daddy driving\
two weeks at the Twelfth\
wife children camera painting things

green worn AA book\
places history A to Z\
stacks of illustrations

passing Ashford Castle Kong
