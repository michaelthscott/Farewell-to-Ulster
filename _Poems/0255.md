wanted to show me something\
and locked the door

wearing shorts like his father\
took his thing up and out

did I know how to\
you do it like this\
standing beside me\
I'm sitting on the toilet seat

boys and still friends but grown \
up since I last saw him \
in Belfast

watching\
no idea what would happen\
then he said come to the bedroom

pushed a chair against the door\
we got into bed

skin made sense\
against stomach\
about to touch

bubble burst

that bloody French boy\
on language exchange\

what are you doing

nothing

when home\
