title: Ashtray
---
Granny Rip's ashtray\
terracotta windowsill

Chinese lady sits\
curl round basket

Granny's ashtray\
in her thin hand

wrinkled fingertip\
tapping silver ash

twenty Sterling\
forty fags a day

after all died of TB
