<h1 class="post-title">Yippie</h1>
</header>

look in the TV\
they're burning the hippie

never got to be one\
already the hippie is dead

long live the yippie

what is a yippie\
a yippie's a hippie but red

look in the TV\
Abbie Hoffman says the hippie is dead
