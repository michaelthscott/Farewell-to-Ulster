<h1 class="post-title">The Machine Stops</h1>
</header>

hint in the \
school curriculum \
The Machine Stops

The barbaric herded horde

gormless git

long jump high jump\
they’re my sports\
but Robert Tavener is taller

Gerry, Harold and Norman\
three brothers

who talks to children\
to find out what they are thinking

why go to school
