<h1 class="post-title">Things I know about this house</h1>
</header>

the darkness of a cupboard\
when it's closed

the feel of the Welsh blanket\
that covers the bed

the way the ornaments\
just sit around

various types of floor\
under various types of carpet

the hinges on the door

ice cold earthenware tiles\
in the hallway

the creeper cut back\
from around the upstairs\
bathroom toilet windows

afternoon spent carving out the\
rot from those selfsame windows

who cleans the toilet bowl

there's a bristly brush on a\
long bamboo pole in\
the toilet in the hall

it's for cleaning cobwebs\
from the corners of a room\
but rarely used

there's probably nothing in this house\
I don't know about

Mummy's drawer\
Daddy's drawer

the cupboards in their bedroom
