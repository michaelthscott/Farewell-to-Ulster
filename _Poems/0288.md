<h1 class="post-title">Family slide show</h1>
</header>

Kodachrome yellow envelope\
horray the slides have come

35mm colour transparencies\
card mounts with red Kodak \
curled up yellow corner

organised by carousel\
old holiday moments\
the boys growing up
