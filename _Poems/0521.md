<h1 class="post-title">North</h1>
</header>

in the north east corner\
of the island beyond\
the other island\
lurks a town \
called Belfast

where men and women\
make children and\
fight and go to church

why I am here is\
inexplicable\
except for the obvious\
explanation that\
I am one of them\
I am one of them because\
some of them\
get out

housing estate against the hill\
says run

dream bullets in the chest

Larne Stranrar\
windy forgot my passport\
looking at the sea\
there and back\
on deck

I can only be one of them\
when I get out
