<h1 class="post-title">North</h1>
</header>

in the north east corner\
of the island beyond\
the other island\
lurks a town \
Belfast

where men and women\
make children fight \
and go to church

why I am here \
is inexplicable\
except for the \
obvious explanation\
that I am one of them

or I am one of them \
because some\
get out

housing estate \
against the hill\
says run

dream bullets in the chest

windy Larne Stranrar\
forgot my passport\
looking at the sea\
there and back\
still on deck

can only be one of them\
when I get out
