<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Piece work</h1>
</header>

snip off right length of ribbon\
fold it down into a bow\
stick pin through the middle\
toss it onto the pile

layout hanky flat in the box\
pin in the corner the bow\
jiggle on acetate lid\
add to wobbly pile
