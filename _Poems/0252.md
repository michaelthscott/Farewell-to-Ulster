<h1 class="post-title">Easy Rider</h1>
</header>

uniform\
wall of Inst\
emits a skinny truant

tieless white nylon shirt\
black blazer grey trousers\
black shoes grey socks

old brown leather satchel\
without its straps

Great Victoria Street\
Odeon Opera House ABC\
empty foyer carpet hush

get your motor running
