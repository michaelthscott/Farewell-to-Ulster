down in town\
they talk like that\
thick as a brick accent

way out here\
on the Hillhall Road\
we're posh but not hoity toity

Mum and Dad\
fled the Malone Road\
golf clubs bridge and tennis

country boy \
with roots in town\
green belt garden paradise
