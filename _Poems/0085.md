<h1 class="post-title">Rupert Stanley College of Higher Education</h1>
</header>

down a side street\
across a car park\
it's all normal

long dull building\
tall thin silent ex-Inst boy\
doing A Levels again

Bay City Rollers \
two haircut brothers\
friendly married girl
