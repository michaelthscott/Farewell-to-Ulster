<h1 class="post-title">Alphabet soup</h1>
</header>

art school\
foundation year\
row of portacabins

walking down \
to the portacabins

stepping out the \
back of the portacabin

white alphabet soup in the sky

lie down at the foot \
of the hawthorn tree
