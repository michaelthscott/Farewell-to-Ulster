their living room is dark\
big old table at the end\
along a low leafy window

lace mats deep dark wood\
rattling little drawer handle

sideboards armchair sofas\
tall writing desk cupboard

high eagle frame mirror\
one little ball missing\
replaced by a bead

fisheye view of the room
