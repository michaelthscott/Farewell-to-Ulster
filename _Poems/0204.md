<h1 class="post-title">Model Shop</h1>
</header>

counter of the Model Shop\
roof where I dream walked\
parallel boy in two worlds

balsa please\
thick medium thin\
flip featherweight stack

always keep the cap on\
whiff quick-dry cement

craft knife blades \
crunch across \
slice along \
the grain
