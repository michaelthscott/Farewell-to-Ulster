<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Inspection</h1>
</header>

Noel took me\
for the once over

that big hotel in Dublin\
Pappy everyone there

I was nervous\
but apparently \
passed muster

they wanted to see \
what kind of girl\
Noel wanted\
to marry
