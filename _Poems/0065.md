<h1 class="post-title">Field trip</h1>
</header>

off on a field trip to Dublin\
sixth formers take over the train\
let's split and track down some vodka\
completely plastered \
on the pavement\
in the rain

puked all the way back to Belfast\
blacked out \
front row \
QFT\
wake up\
tail end of credits\
Henry Miller’s Quiet Days in Clichy
