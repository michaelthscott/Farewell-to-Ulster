<h1 class="post-title">Lord of the Rings</h1>
</header>

deep in a bubble of blankets and sheets\
my goggle-eyed torch \
feeding mind

eating up letters \
of the words of the lines of the pages

each curled at the corner\
progressivelly thumbed

a map and a compass on the cover
