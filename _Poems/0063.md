title: Porn
---
not in a shop from a woman\
maybe have to queue

from an old man\
newspaper kiosk

i know the one\
covered in magazines\
boobs hidden round the side

pick and pay

Fiesta\
two and six\
folded\
in my school bag\
didn't even look at me
