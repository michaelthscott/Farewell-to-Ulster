title: The Jungfrau Mountain
---
nestled in our campsite Grindlewald\
bulks of rock scrape summit lines

we're clunking up the North Face inside\
viewing window family made adventure

beyond the Eiger \
taller yet is spoken of

how can a mountain be just that\
Young Girl in the spraken deutch
