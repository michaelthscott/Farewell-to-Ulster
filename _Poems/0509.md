<h1 class="post-title">Let it burn</h1>
</header>

them and us\
the teachers\
school rules\
hair too long

Mr Auld's divinity class

a man on his own\
with a smart little MG\
and a cottage in Portrush\
where he takes some \
boys not me

they say he was a reverend\
relaxed openminded classes\
but such a useless subject

Gauguin's La Source\
hung in his old gloomy room\
before he moved upstairs\
to round tables bright

that old room was where\
I was going to burn\
the school\
down

candle in a saucer\
petrol sloshed about
