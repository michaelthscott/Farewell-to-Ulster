<h1 class="post-title">Let it burn</h1>
</header>

them and us\
the teachers\
school rules\
long hair\
Mr Auld's divinity class\
a man on his own\
with a little MG\
and a cottage in Portrush\
he takes some boys to\
they say he was a reverend\
relaxed almost openminded classes\
such a useless subject

Gauguin's La Source\
hung in hs old gloomy room\
before he moved upstairs\
round tables\
bright

that old room was where\
i was going to burn\
the school\
down

with a candle\
in a saucer\
petrol sloshed about
