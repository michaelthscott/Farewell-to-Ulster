<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Alfred Jarry</h1>
</header>

Simms pins up a dandy \
longhair arrogant stare\
called homosexual fart

cycling towpaths\
fishing the Seine\
outskirts of Paris

lives in a hut\
his Pyramid

pataphysical\
penniless\
drinker
