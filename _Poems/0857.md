title: Alfred Jarry
---
Simms pins up a dandy \
longhair arrogant stare\
called homosexual fart

cycling towpaths\
fishing the Seine\
outskirts of Paris

lives in a hut\
his Pyramid

pataphysical\
penniless\
drinker
