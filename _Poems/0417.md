<h1 class="post-title">Gooseberry picking</h1>
</header>

goosegabs got Mum’s thumb\
stabbed her jabbed her cuticle

it’s gone septic all swoll up\
I have to help her

gauze tube over metal thing\
twist it push it tie wrist strap

protected by a leather sheath
