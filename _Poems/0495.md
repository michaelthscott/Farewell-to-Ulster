Heath Drive\
London\
Gordon Bull\
again\
my friend

but older\
living in London now\
big city boy\
country boy\
awkward country boy\
slightly left out

old low buildings\
over the railway tracks\
Finchley Road

the bedroom incident\
the bedroom chair against the door\
the French boy pushing against it\
we leap out of bed
