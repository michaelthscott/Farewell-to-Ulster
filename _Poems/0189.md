<h1 class="post-title">Swimming lesson</h1>
</header>

in our cool blue school pool\
I can swim the whole length\
underwater

silent bursting \
last strokes \
to the wall

then suddenly the refrain \
in the changing room

Scott's got a hard on!

but I haven't
