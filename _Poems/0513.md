<h1 class="post-title">Modelmaking</h1>
</header>

a model is like a thing\
a small thing like\
a bigger thing

a model\
a smaller thing\
a bigger thing\
alike

toy train\
toy car\
toy plane\
toy soldier

sometimes a model needs a base\
a little bit of ground\
around a house

war gamers model trenches\
model earth\
shell holes\
they calculate with rulers\
tape measures\
the distance of the guns\
shell burst\
roll the dice\
the damage\
soldiers dead

my toy theatre\
has layers\
that come together\
make sense\
when seen from the front
