snowing in April\
dark cubbyhole

shelves behind shared bed\
full of old volumes \
of Punch

engraved cartoons\
old characters\
and jokes

Mum persuaded Lady Rowan\
to lend us the stable house\
behind Tollymore carpark

my blue and white wooly hat \
sprouts its fluffy flat tassel

I'm wearing it next to \
Gordon in bed
