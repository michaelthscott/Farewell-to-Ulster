<h1 class="post-title">Cub Scouts</h1>
</header>

Cubs are little Scouts\
we're green with caps\
long socks and garters

Scout uniform has changed\
allowed long trousers now\
no more Mountie hat\
badges look cheaper \
plastic not embroidered

dib dib dib\
dob dob dob

lining up\
rushing about\
scarf with a toggle
