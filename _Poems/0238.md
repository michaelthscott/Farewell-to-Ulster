<h1 class="post-title">Radio</h1>
</header>

rounded tone voices\
dialect everyday drama\
blanket me wrapped up in bed\
off school sick

blue vegetable peeling bowl\
thermometer\
keep it under your tongue\
sleeping in the middle of the day\
the Home Service
