<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Longshot</h1>
</header>

Grandpa Rip gave us\
a copper roof low flat\
and bluey green

when I climb up the \
ladder that I unhook \
and pull down I go\
up into the roof

can't stand up in it

careful don't stand \
between the joists\
go through the ceiling\
only plasterboard

some things close\
things we need\
like suitcases\
cardboard boxes \
full of dalia bulbs

Daddy's paintings\
stacked\
against each other\
leaned against beams

there's the old castle\
Studio International\
the dressing up box

the places in a house\
which are not rooms
