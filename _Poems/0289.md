<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Fruit and vegetables</h1>
</header>

time makes it summer again\
weeks and weeks of weekend \
giving back the garden \
to aimlessly play in\
 \
casual inspection\
of strawberry plants\
soil covered straw mats\
rows that move each year\
rotating with the potatoes

never occurs to me why \
never recruited in \
adult world

three sons polluted \
by not here forever \
Longshot
