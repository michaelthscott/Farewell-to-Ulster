title: Daddy’s parents
---
Grandpa Scott's moustache\
white bristly and wide

right in the middle of his face\
curls up half hides his Harold smile

Granny Scott's buck teeth\
not so much you'd notice

she raps his knuckles\
with the back of a spoon

stop fidgeting!
