<h1 class="post-title">Basil Blackshaw</h1>
</header>

I'm trying to paint \
a Basil Blackshaw \
hill full of silage

solitary hawthorn \
against silver sky

over the hill comes a tractor\
methodically cutting grass \
right before my very eye
