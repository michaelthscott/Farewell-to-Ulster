<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Sideburns</h1>
</header>

proud of his farmer prince sideburns\
fingertips always parting his hair

two Michaels stand \
shoulder to shoulder

does she love him\
I don't really care

where's Michael\
didn't you hear\
he was in a car crash

set off in a panic to Antrim\
thinking only how to get there

feel like a fool in the hospital\
to be met with a melancholy stare
