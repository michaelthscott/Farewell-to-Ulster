<h1 class="post-title">Chimney sweep</h1>
</header>

old newspaper sheets\
around the hearth

blackened smell of soot

bristle fan brush\
shoved roughly up \
screwing bendy rods

grime lined silent face of coal\
was long gone chimney sweep
