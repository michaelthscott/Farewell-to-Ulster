<h1 class="post-title">bushy hairy</h1>
</header>

my father pulls very slowly\
the left side of his moustache

it's getting longer at the sides\
it's almost a Viva Zapata!

slowly his sideburns \
became muttonchop

keeps a short comb \
to lay down the silver \
wing behind his ear

not bohemian\
artistic
