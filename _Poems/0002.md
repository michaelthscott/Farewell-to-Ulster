<h1 class="post-title">Foundation</h1>
</header>

Art Foundation Jordanstown\
out at the Ulster Polytechnic

down from portacabin backdoor\
playing field stretching sky\
clouds of alphabet soup

tripping tripping\
everything is slipping\
mind finds totally inside out
