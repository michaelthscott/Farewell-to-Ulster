title: Father's friend
---
George and Peter Ellis\
why is she called Peter

George and Dad\
on holiday together\
in their little sports car

occasionally we'd visit\
them on the Malone Road\
my odd feeling of entering\
other people's houses

I like Caroline\
a year older than me\
helps box hankies\
and we laugh

George died \
on the beach in Italy

that was the end of that
