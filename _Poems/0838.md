title: Work
---
neither Grandpa works\
that's because they're so old\
they're retired

neither Granny works\
that's because they stay at home\
they're housewives

meanwhile Daddy treks\
back and forwards to the factory

and Mummy went back to teaching\
so she could buy her Mini
