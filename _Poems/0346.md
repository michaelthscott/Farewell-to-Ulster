<h1 class="post-title">Incident</h1>
</header>

this old bald Scottish guy\
in the cafe me looking at\
him as he talks and my\
mind is saying I could kiss\
him why that I don't know

and then he takes me to his\
sea front terraced house\
in Gatehouse of Fleet\
my wife's away

gets me oh so drunk\
on whiskey and then\
bed and he's why not\
just saying he'll get in\
beside me

in the morning when he is out\
getting stuff for breakfast\
I check his wardrobe\
no wife's clothes

next it's the guy who drifts \
his hand from gear stick\
to touch my leg\
let me out

I so missed Simms and \
Speedy that night sleeping \
in the roundabout long grass\
where that slimey groper left me

call on John Wade in London\
go to some writer's flat\
typed manuscript of \
meaningless \
sentences

two tickets for the Isle of Wight\
festival in my pocket but go\
back home to Belfast\
miss Jimi Hendrix

find Simms has \
gone to Turkey\
without me
