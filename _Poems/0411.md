<h1 class="post-title">Thugs</h1>
</header>

Brittany must be like Donegal

that jetty where he twisted \
his ankle in the fight is the \
same old cobble stone pier

no Gauguin in Donegal \
Roundstone or Belmullet

that's France Europe \
where that happens

though we got \
thugs \
in Belfast too
