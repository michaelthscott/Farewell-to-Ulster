<h1 class="post-title">Hymnal</h1>
</header>

pick up dark red hymn book

corners turned inward \
against \
fingered gilt edged pages

thin ribbon hanging out \
frayed end \
soft little demon tongue

pause everyone\
finding the page\
of the next hymn

last breath\
of the knee cushion\
when we go down to kneel

Reverend sermon Upritchard\
stealth bomb usually amuses

soars towards\
lands on\
idea
