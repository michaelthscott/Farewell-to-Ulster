title: Garden nest
---
leave the nest\
leave the nest\
as if the nest would stay

beginner \
here in time\
nothing happens\
no one notices what happens\
who notices what happens in a garden

somethings the gardener notices\
somethings the busy gardener ignores\
somethings the lazy cat and boy enjoy
