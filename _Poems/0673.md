<h1 class="post-title">Prisoner of Inst</h1>
</header>

people animals \
insects and birds\
buildings machines

plants trees \
bushes and grass

empty space \
for sky and clouds

rain snow wind\
litter exhaust \
traffic noise

leaving Ballyaughlis\
the Ballylesson Road\
goes down a steep hill

there's big trees\
entrance to a big house hidden

view of fields\
a footpath\
up another hill\
another house\
half hidden house\
footpath and hedge

walk the Ballylesson Road
