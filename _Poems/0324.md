crinkled tinfoil\
homemade lid\
stained old stainless steel spoon\
opened cat food tin\
with a couple of unopened tins\
on the bottom\
on the left\
in the pantry\
feed the cat

did you feed the cat\
will you feed the cat\
I already fed the cat\
did you give it some milk\
wild animal\
tame animal\
meat on the bone\
smelly brown chunks and jelly\
in a tin

smelly brown nuggets\
shake out of a box

sniffy\
turning away from his food
