<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Double declutch</h1>
</header>

tootling past stone walls \
out of Killybegs town\
Uncle Bonar demos his \
double declutch

cruise into the curve\
did you hear the wheels squeek\
sure old Jemimah \
she's as good as \
an MG
