<h1 class="post-title">Comics</h1>
</header>

cheap things\
not made to last \
poor quality paper\
read them fast

get Eagle yes every week\
Dan Dare against The Mekon\
but no Beano no Beano no\
not Dandy either

black and white colour world\
scan speech bubble strips

later to dabble in\
Marvel's real tales\
already surpassed
