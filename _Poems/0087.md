title: Connemara
---
seaside\
run and hide

rock pool\
water's cool

starfish\
make a wish

seaweed \
book to read

big shell\
listen well

cold wave\
stone to save

sunbeam\
dam the stream

driftwood\
time for food

picnic\
play a trick

sand dune\
sing a tune

dry bone\
driving home
