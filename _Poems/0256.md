<h1 class="post-title">Handlebars</h1>
</header>

handlebar connected to the \
hand bone

hand bone connected to the \
steering bone

steering bone disconnected from the \
thinking bone

here's what's going on in my mind

i'm in a swamp naked captured\
by beautiful naked amazons\
boys like me but pure sex

they've tied me \
put me in their reed prison \
I wait naked and dirty and tied

they will come \
and suck me and fuck me \
rub all over me with oils and mud

I'm as hard as a rock\
and loving every minute\
safe forever in their hands

they're so friendly\
I eventually get to join their gang \
no one gets older \
we capture other boys\
and play the same game

fucking and sucking \
them in our prison \
then letting them join the gang

forever and ever and ever\
bumping down the toepath\
up the left bank of the Lagan
