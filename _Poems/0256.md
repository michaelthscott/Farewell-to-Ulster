<h1 class="post-title">Handlebars</h1>
</header>

handlebar connected to the \
hand bone

hand bone connected to the \
steering bone

steering bone disconnected from the \
thinking bone

here's what's going on in my mind

in a swamp naked captured\
by beautiful naked amazons

wait in their reed prison \
naked dirty and tied

they will come \
play torture with me\
safe forever in their hands

so friendly I eventually \
get to join their gang

capture others\
no one gets older \
play the same game

bumping down the toepath\
along the side of the Lagan
