<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Foundation year</h1>
</header>

Che beard\
from England\
sweet Pete Hardie\
teaching Foundation Year

was at Hornsea during the sit-in\
now portakabins by the playing field

quiet in his little room\
he can seem sad\
stuck with us\
here in NI
