<h1 class="post-title">Knockbreda High School</h1>
</header>

me and Harry Nugent\
drawn together\
by inertia

edge of a housing estate\
rubbish bin tarmac\
stuck

a pair of disconnects\
outside the local shop

aimless lunch break discussions
