<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Badminton</h1>
</header>

delicate shuttlecock\
hard tensed strings

changing

white lines\
church hall floor

zipping up the racket cover

waiting on the pavement\
afterwards\
for Daddy to pick us up\
in the car
