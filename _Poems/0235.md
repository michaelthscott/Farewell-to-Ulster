<h1 class="post-title">Mr Jacob Bronowski</h1>

applause\
bla bla bla\
The Ascent of Man\
bla bla

you see I see ideas\
as shapes\
in shapes\
through shapes\
in three dimensions

nothing odd in that\
why sounds\
so strange\
to them

Parky\
respectful attentive\
listening to little old thinker man
