<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Long hair</h1>
</header>

first day back\
middle of the quad\
Erwin says\
you growing your hair long

it’s not that long\
barely covers my ears

how did I not notice\
how did I manage to \
get away with it

pull it forward\
over my ear
