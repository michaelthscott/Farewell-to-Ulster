<h1 class="post-title">Goethe's Theory of Colour</h1>
</header>

choice spot in the garden\
to lie and bask in the sun

never used path by the fence\
at the end of the beech hedge

next door's paddock long grass\
sad horse of invisible Mr Spring

least used lawn between \
big flower bed and rockery

primary secondary\
complimentary sun
