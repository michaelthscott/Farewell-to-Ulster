every school day bus day\
pass through Dunmurray

grey lower middle class land\
we never stop there except

occasionally stop to pick up\
something at the Dub Stores

probably some gang round here\
lurk in that banal housing estate

beyond an empty sports field\
ghostly distant grey mansion

Fleming Fulton School\
for the handicapped

where Julie goes
