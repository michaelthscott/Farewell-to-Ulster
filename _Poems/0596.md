<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Escape routes</h1>
</header>

New Block\
no way out \
that spiked gate

Black Man \
best not out\
the front gate

slip out\
low wall\
side gate

step by step

tie off \
solo

Belfast city centre
