title: Cat food
---
smelly brown jelly\
with livery chunks\
never ever taste it

clever new tin opener\
cut the side of the tin\
no more ragged edge

stained special spoon\
use only for cat food \
store in the cupboard

small plastic blue bowl\
living under the trolley \
cat fed family animal
