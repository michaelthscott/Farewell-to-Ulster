<h1 class="post-title">Drums</h1>
</header>

dumb dumb dumb\
bellow your Lambeg drum\
Glorious Twelth grumble\
over Cave Hill

Orange bloody Order\
God bless the Battle of The Boyne\
rival religious regiments \
always ready to kill
