<h1 class="post-title">Our garden</h1>
</header>

the garden is a map of use

there's not a square inch \
I haven't been in seen \
or crawled through

the front lawn of wobbly bicycle

the little corner down near the gate \
too small to do anything with \
but keep neat

the other side at the front\
where the bank is steep\
and it floods

sheets of ice in winter\
dark and damp

warm inside the pile of cut grass
