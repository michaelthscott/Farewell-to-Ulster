<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Innuendo</h1>
</header>

lined up\
squatting\
backs along the wall\
nudge nod and wink

recorder stuck\
between knees\
waggling

didn't see it as funny\
until explained

bunch of sniggering gits
