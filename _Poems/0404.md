<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Neighbours</h1>
</header>

Cecil Raphael's \
gleaming cannonball \
we know from church

the McDowells \
seem barely polite

that new family \
with the monster house\
are trouble makers for sure

only Scotts and Dorritys combine

sullen fences hedges gates\
family life of spiders\
driving cars
