<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Sunday papers</h1>
</header>

dressed for church\
church finished

crunch of car park stones\
the mystery of Mary Rippingham

pick up the the sunday papers\
dark low cottage shop\
open on a sunday morning

thick wad of newsprint\
folded to hold the colour supplement
