Timothy's old guitar\
never played\
is bust

only got one string\
the lowest one

bumpy metal wire\
can run my nail along\
 \
tape recorder microphone \
shoved under like a pickup

fill blank cassettes with\
rip your ears off sound

I'll never \
live in town\
be in a band
