<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Our cat</h1>
</header>

a cat's claws\
get worn\
a little bit \
at the tip

this cat's fur \
goes shiny flat when stroked

this cat's nose\
is dry

this cat's chin is droolably wet

any cat's tail\
is rightly\
suddenly dangerous

no cat's tooth\
should be\
broken
