<h1 class="post-title">Old art magazines</h1>
</header>

browse Studio International\
ignore dull too old The Artist

contemporary beats traditional\
bundled in the attic

art abstract\
art stylish\
art design

bold colours\
simple shapes\
pure American

art landscape\
bowls of fruit\
naked women curves

yes I know where\
there are photos\
of naked women\
tiny in this house
