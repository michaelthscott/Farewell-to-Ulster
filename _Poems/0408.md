I could tell he didn't like it\
my crude Leger flower

he did a rose\
and I thought\
yes that's more artistic

someone might like to buy that bag\
simple brown hessian bag with a black rose

but actually neither of us\
knows much about\
selling anything

or if we do\
circumstance wouldn't\
show it
