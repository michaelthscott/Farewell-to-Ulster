title: Me
---
I am who am I am he\
skinny lanky silly me\
feel it coming thicken feel\
finger thumb light lost and he\
is silly lanky skinny me\
back it fades back to forgotten\
silly me I see lonely me me me me me\
me me me me me me\
me me\
me\
silly\
me
