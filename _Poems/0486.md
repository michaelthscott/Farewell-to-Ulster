<h1 class="post-title">Sketch book</h1>
</header>

black book\
used for writing\
page forms an idea

could I be a writer

no not one to tell\
story end to end

painting words\
poetry too dull

better like cinema\
should be exciting

first Kerouac \
now Burroughs
