<h1 class="post-title">I Ching</h1>
</header>

just beside the bank\
where Dad drops off\
the cheques each day\
to prevent the overdraft\
from going over and \
bankrupting the Factory

just beside the bank\
is Solly Lipschitz's shop\
second hand records\
America Europe\
trumpet saxaphone \
bass and drums

America's thought of itself\
its emptiness\
it's what there is to learn\
but it doesn't really interest me\
I'm buying my first copy of the I Ching
