<h1 class="post-title">Turn on tune in drop out</h1>
</header>

just beside the bank\
where Dad drops off\
the cheques each day\
to prevent the overdraft\
bankrupting the factory

just beside the bank\
Solly Lipschitz's shop\
second hand records\
American European \
trumpet saxaphone \
bass and drums

America mirrors itself\
its emptiness its accent\
its endless entertainment\
it's what there is to learn

Dice Man buys I Ching
