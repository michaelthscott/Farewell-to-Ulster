Monopoly on the carpet\
Go Risk Cludo Totopoly\
the rules\
the start\
the picking up the card\
the dice roll\
my special tiny silver top hat\
slightly badly cast\
triumph\
rows of red hotels\
down Mayfair Park Lane\
London Town\
counting money
