title: Ritual
---
gothic carved \
hymnal numbers board \
for us to follow rising into song

creaks and squeaks of\
those leather cushions \
we kneel on sniggering

occasionally for the wine and bread\
Mum would go up to the altar rail\
but only her we didn’t move\
Daddy too stayed \
behind why
