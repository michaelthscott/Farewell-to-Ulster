<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">No more school</h1>
</header>

uniform gone\
hair cut

something is supposed to happen\
from art college to career

knowing nothing\
assuming the world is full\
assuming that the world is fertile\
assuming that the world will raise me up

recognise and raise me up
