<h1 class="post-title">Portrait</h1>
</header>

Inchmarlo lawn\
portraits drawn\
it's an artist\
Mr Knox

in you go\
make do studio\
posed in silent\
white tent

mouth seems right\
chin's too big\
above the TV set\
Michael in olde script
