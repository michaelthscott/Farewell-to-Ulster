<h1 class="post-title">Playroom window</h1>
</header>

some old bits of wood\
became a bird table\
some old tins of paint\
painted it\
brown and green\
like a sculpture\
not a bird table

outside the playroom window

standing by the TV\
in front of the window\
looking down the drive\
looking at the bird table\
like a sculpture\
waiting for the results to come\
from Chelsea
