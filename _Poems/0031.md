<h1 class="post-title">Lugar</h1>
</header>

glinty crackled chrome lost in larch needles\
it's my Lugar

wire brush it back to authentic dull metal grey\
it's my gun again

bendy breastplate sword and silly helmet\
red green hat of Robin Hood

in my bedroom two long barrelled Colts\
in their special box

a real expensive catapult with tailored leather grip\
could kill a bird
