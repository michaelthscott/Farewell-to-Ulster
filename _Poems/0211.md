<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">Where Daddy works</h1>
</header>

hessian bales\
grease in machinery\
big spaces\
old floors\
typewriter\
ink pads and stamps\
in the office

family business\
Grandpa's brothers sons\
Barron & Co.\
this is where Daddy works\
he is a manager

your father went into\
the factory at fifteen

never asked why
