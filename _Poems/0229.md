<header class="post-header">
<h1 class="post-title">White Island</h1>
</header>

dropped me off at the jetty \
alone to do some fishing

devil goat appears

I retreat to the ruin\
clamber to the top

watch it relentlessly\
head butt the wall

menacing\
persistent

like an enemy from school
