<h1 class="post-title">Time and The Conways</h1>
</header>

Mum says he has nice crow's feet\
wrinkle crinkles around his eyes\
I’m surprised she said that\
need to ask what it means

intermission \
at the Arts Theatre\
why does she like wrinkles\
around his eyes
