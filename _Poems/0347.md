title: Freak out
---
White Heat White Light\
showing off to each other

endless thudding Sister Ray \
noise no lights chaos

unregulated afternoon\
chaos cynicism spreads\
disrespect for college property

mucking about larking about\
psychadelic light show

messing about mucking about \
stoned it's a Happening

trio follower manipulator admirer
