title: Society of Friends
---
Dorothy Brittan\
at home behind her son's house\
like a Granny Scott replacement

more obviously loving\
plus she's a Quaker

why are we not Quakers Mum\
they don't have a service\
don't sing hymns

the Barritts too are Quakers
