title: Uncle Norman
---
Uncle Norman wears a\
bright red handkerchief\
in his breast pocket

baggy old tweed\
jacket makes him\
seem so bohemian

but actually he's as\
indifferent and distant\
as an old stone wall

he's a fisherman but\
there's something dandified\
less practical than Harold

like he's snobbish\
savouring tackle\
more than fish

and his hawkish daughter\
Ann disparages with\
her brittle pride

too Malone Road the both of them
