<h1 class="post-title">Beauty</h1>
</header>

in the corner window \
opposite the Black Man

pure sky blue\
lambswool \
polo neck

oh it is so beautiful\
can see myself in that

drag Mum got to get it \
face shadowy hesitation \
explaining it’s for ladies

want it\
buy it anyway

but never wore it much\
rollneck too high
