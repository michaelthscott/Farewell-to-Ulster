ok I’m not that smart\
but I’m definitely not that stupid\
so tell me why am I in 3E2

actually no one seems to wonder\
no one seems to care\
A B C or D\
what difference\
I don't care

where's the career advisor\
what happens after school

I’m a sausage\
in a sausage factory
