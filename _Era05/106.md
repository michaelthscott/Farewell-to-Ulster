title: The playroom
collection: Era05

early off to school breakfasts
still dark outside

one big three small armchairs
one on the side

bright brick red table
folded away

two built in cupboards
jampacked with stuff

Waddington games
shoe cleaning stuff

Granny's old china
squirreled away

rolled up searsucker naptkins
five naptkin rings

in the corner sits the telly

transcendent
evening centre of things
