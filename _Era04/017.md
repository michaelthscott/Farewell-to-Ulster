just the right height \
to clamber through rhododendron

just the right age \
to wait watching trout

the Shimna river has \
pools of clear glass

my secret wee tiddlers\
dart and fall back

up here i am the cat\
untraceable child of the jungle

silent and perched \
on a big boulder cliff

sending twigs to the sea\
down the Shimna

Shimna Shimna\
Tollymore Park

Shimna Shimna\
Mourne mountain rain

how many hours \
have I been here \
with my chin on my knees
