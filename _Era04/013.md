tiger cubs squabble\
tumble endlessly\
on the backseat

suntanned arm \
reaches back\
to say stop

you're distracting \
your father while \
he is driving us

I-spy with my little eye \
something beginning \
with now let me see

autoroute turns \
into autobahn\
motorways

yoghurt \
new cheese\
and apfelsaft

white lines float \
in the sunshine\
nose on glass
