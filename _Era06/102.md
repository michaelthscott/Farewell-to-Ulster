Barry has a bit of asthma 
but Trevor Munn is worse

thin wheezy laugh
squeezed out 
from inside 
his blazer

dry dusty 
flakey skin

pale weak feathery
orphan eyes in prison
wistfully giving up hope

watch three chatty boys
Erwin and Trevor and me
around the woodwork table

did you both hear it I love it 
I'm Sorry I'll Read That Again
