title: Our parents' bedroom
series: Era06

Mum and Dad\
double bed

Mummy on the right\
Daddy on the left

a mat each side they made\
from Matisse leaf shapes

cupboards on Mummy's side\
chest of drawers on Daddy's

two identical bedside tables\
honeymoon visit to Heal's

Mummy's dressing table \
angled between two windows

three moveable mirrors\
careful put them back as they were

the other chest of drawers\
full of Mum's things underwear

as you come in\
beside the door

the little armchair\
beside the upstairs phone

Daddy's bookshelves\
holiday slides in boxes

and the big trunk on short legs\
contains Daddy's painting things

savour turpentine and linseed oil\
latch the snap back catches

behind the bed\
their homemade headboard

beneath the carpet\
original floor boards
