summer saturday\
coming in the kitchen door\
and the old grey cast iron pot\
is on a swirling boil

Dylon tin punctured open\
the shiny metal green\
of bright dye yellow water

nylon white shirt\
twisted rolled knotted\
into the pot we go

with old grey wooden\
laundry tongs from the \
days of the mangle

I like the green and yellow\
better than the red and blue

look carefully\
schoolboy collar never change

out now on the hangar\
hippy tie dye festival

short lived summer\
took so long\
but gone
