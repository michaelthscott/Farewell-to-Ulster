this old bald Scottish guy
in the cafe me looking at
him as he talks and my
mind is saying I could kiss
him why that i don't know

and then he takes me to his
sea front terraced house in
Gatehouse of Fleet
my wife's away
and gets me oh so drunk
on whiskey and then in
bed and he is why not
just saying he'll get in
beside me

in the morning when he was
out getting stuff for breakfast
I checked his wardrobe
no wife's clothes

next it was the guy who
drifted his hand from gear stick
to my leg
let me out

god I missed Simms and Speedy
that night sleeping in the
long grass of the roundabout
where that slimey groper
had left me
so lonely

called on John Wade in London
went to some writer's flat
manuscript of meaningless sentences

two tickets for the Isle of Wight
festival in my pocket but
I went back home to Belfast
missed Jimi Hendrix

Simms has gone to Turkey
without me
