---
title: Unused room
---
when Grandpa Rip
planned this house
he made a big living room

parquet floors
french windows
fireplace
but it's cold

and Mummy likes to keep it clean

so we sit in the playroom
at the front
radiator television
fold away table
every night the BBC news

it freaks me out
to see us all like zombies
glued to the goggle box

in the living room
on the sofa

on Granny Scott's fox furs
that Timothy sewed together

i'm reading
Shamanism by
Mircea Eliade
