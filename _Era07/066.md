Heysham to Hull\
cycle sixty miles\
realising I'm red in \
the campsite mirror\
whole side of my face

Hull Rotterdam ferry\
bunch of us cyclists \
trying to find our way \
out of the dock

Dutchman\
long black hair\
exploded hot damn!\
in an American accent

make it to Amsterdam\
tunnel under the river\
campsite night\
guitar strum\
there is a\
house

Stedelijk\
oh my god!\
American shouts\
entering the room\
full of Sunflowers

Kroller Muller amid sand dunes \
many Gauguins from Moscow\
street corner Yaqui sorcerer\
in swivel paperpack stand

south now towards Liege\
halfway up a huge hill\
stopping in a bar\
order Stella

guided into Brussels and\
deposited in a back garden by\
two anonymous boys on bicycles

standing in Paris by a fountain\
this priest comes up to me\
starts talking in French\
says he thought\
I must be\
French

I’m in a campsite\
in out from Robinson\
the long empty platform\
look at Paris from the train

alone walking around\
empty suburbs of\
Impressionists

the day I went to Versailles\
sort of lost along the edge\
of the dual carriageway

small insignificant speck\
can drift anywhere
