finally they paid off
the mortgage for 
the field

that farm house you can see
in the distance on the hill
against the tree
like the house
i used to stare at
in the curtain
old London
fabric from Heals

Mr McCormick
who sold
Mum and Dad
the land

the hay machine

we live in the countryside
but we're not people of the land

farmers
gardeners
