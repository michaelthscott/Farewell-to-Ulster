collection: Era07

in the junk shop 
on the corner of Shaftsbury Square
a pile of walking sticks by the door

tall one with a demon head
lopsided snarl

Chee-Foo Shan
scratched below

now my Gauguin walking stick

in the garage
clipping copper
fashioning a ferulle
