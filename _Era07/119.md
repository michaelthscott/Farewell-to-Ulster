---
title: Swing
---
we've got a swing in our garden
we can swing on it 
seven days a week

of course we don't use it that often
but when we do
it never fails to squeak

sometimes I look up at Orion
and from the Plough 
pick out the North Pole star

we're on the ground 
but through the sky 
we're flying

who knows what 
or where or why 
we really are
