Mummy's always busy\
Daddy's usually tired\
weekdays that is

Saturday Sunday\
he's out in the garden\
or taking us somewhere in the car

sometimes in evening he reads\
or organises holiday slides

Mummy brings things in off the line\
takes the ironing up to the hot press

Mummy's always busy\
until early to bed and reads

Daddy has a late night cigarette\
watching the end of a movie with me

don't forget to put the cat out\
don't forget to put the lights out

end of television for today\
long beep to wake people up

so earth ball spins
