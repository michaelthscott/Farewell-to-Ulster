missed the bus\
so I'll walk home\
pavement of Great Victoria Street

round Shaftsbury Square\
there's no one there\
couple of drumsticks on a building

Sammy goes South\
walked all the way\
Port Said to Durban

Solly Lipsitz\
plenty of jazz\
Sonny Rollins face on an album

short back and sides\
Dunmurry line\
cross the bridge with my hair long

round the back of Queen's\
slip into dreams\
what if I was epileptic

Methodist College\
rival of Inst\
telltale tie in my pocket

get the six o'clock\
no point to wait\
head on out the Malone Road

called it the shoebox\
where Gordon Bull lived\
before he moved to London

Richmond Lodge\
look for Mum's Mini\
today's not a day she's a teacher

Inchmarlo stop\
mute relic still there\
the pull out stone to sit on
