title: Heysham
series: Era03
---
hurry up Heysham steamer\
creak across the Irish Sea\
chatterbox tenterhooks dreamer\
off on our continental holiday

scamper up from the clanky hold\
scatter round corridors and decks\
wide eyed in the passenger lounge\
wondering whatever comes next

past the Isle of Man she rolls\
climbing bunkbeds brothers three\
leap both feet onto England\
car sticker says were GB
