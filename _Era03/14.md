title: Chain broke
---
Belgian caravan site
in normal daylight
one man tent in the grass

bent over a broken bicycle chain
my young man is learning
resilience again

got to ask them for some tools

yes actually I fixed it
and she
the daughter
and I
took a walk
in the thick shadow pine
did nothing but talk
on a log in a wood
until dark

tall blond girl from Holland
serious girls like me but as a friend
