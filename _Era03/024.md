title: Up the river
series: Era03

let's go down to the river

not a river\
it's a stream

in and out of these bumpy green fields\
really don't know who owns them

in a child sized valley\
down the end of the field\
look that's a meander

buried in brambles \
tunnel of branches and leaves\
glistening wadable splashable floor

bright splash each step\
dark secret crossed branches

splish splash each step\
up the river

branch woven sky\
bramble woven thorns\
damp grassless mud

each splish splash step \
up the river

the steep bit down where we get in\
the open bit with the high bank\
the dark bit with the waterfall

the top where the trees thin out\
becomes swampy

ravine meander embankment\
flood plain debris

cows come down to drink

each splish step splash\
up the river

on our own

each splishy step splash\
up the river
