title: Saturday
---
we reach our height on Saturday night
getting dark finishing up in the garden

Mum 
changing out 
of her gardening clothes
just let me run a brush through my hair

bacon fills the pan
tomatoes cut in half 
bubbles spit boiling oil

whole pan full of frantic frying
whole pan full of bacon curled
whole pan full of potato bread
whole pan full of pairs of eggs

oh a double yoker
whole pan full we made tea
we made Saturday night tea

Daddy 
last in hand washing
hair flat from the wooly hat
his moustache is getting wider

he's a garden man
loves his garden
loves his garden work
