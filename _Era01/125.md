title: Ballyaughlis
---
Ballyaughlis crossroads
pub garage shop
two bus stop

into Belfast
one to school
one coming home

Jimmy McGarry's hair 
slicked like an old rocker
rickety open double doors 
behind ancient Esso pumps

dull crimson corrugated iron
greasy dark navy overalls
naked underneath

put a tiger in your tank

Daddy doesn't use him
Uncle Bonar does
